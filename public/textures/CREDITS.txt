Planet textures
===============

Sun, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn (and rings), Uranus, Neptune:
  Solar System Scope (https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/)
  Licensed CC BY 4.0, based on NASA elevation and imagery data.

Earth (day, normal, specular, clouds, night lights) and Moon:
  three.js examples (https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js) - MIT License.
